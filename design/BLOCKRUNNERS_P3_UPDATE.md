## SECTION D — Talking points for engagement (replies + DMs)

For the marketing team to have canned answers ready:

**"Is this on-chain?"**
> UNATOMS renders directly from Bitcoin block headers — pure SVG, offline-deterministic, verifiable by anyone. Series 0 will be inscribed as ordinals post-graduation from nat.fun (TAKEDMT token). The rendering engine is provably tied to real block bytes, not a random seed.

**"How is this different from other DMT projects?"**
> Most DMT projects use one field (usually block hash) as an entropy source. UNATOMS reads *five* real header fields — height, hash, merkleroot, time, nonce — and each one drives specific parts of the art. It's Digital Matter Theory taken to its actual conclusion: the fields don't just seed the art, they *are* the art.

**"How does rarity work?"**
> Nobody set the rarity. Bitcoin did. We designed which byte drives which trait, then ran it against the first 10,080 blocks and counted what came out. NAT SIGIL landed on 59 pieces (~0.59%). Hi element on 40 (~0.40%). Anyone can regenerate the full rarity sheet offline and verify.

**"What's the Hi element?"**
> An invented rare — but the rule is verifiable. When a block's own hash starts with the same byte as its merkle root (its transaction fingerprint), that block "waves hello." Probability = 1 in 256. Under 40 blocks in the whole collection qualify.

**"What's NAT SIGIL?"**
> The NAT logo re-imagined as a sacred third-eye crown. It only appears on blocks that satisfy specific chain conditions — heights containing "111" plus a rare byte-agreement gate. Roughly 0.6% of the collection. It's the collection's brand-anchor trait.

**"Can I mint one now?"**
> Not yet — we're pre-graduation. Right now $DMT (TakeDMT) is on Solana. Post-graduation on nat.fun, each claim becomes an ordinal-inscribed UNATOM. Get on the list at unatom.fun.

**"Who's Joe?"**
