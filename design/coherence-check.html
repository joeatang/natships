How to read this — each tile is a fully-random UNATOM synthesized from random values across every trait dial. Overlays (NAT SIGIL / flameborn / blazedBluntie / Hi) are forced onto ~15% of tiles each so composability is stress-tested too. Real production combos will look CLEANER than these because they follow byte-derived distributions, not pure random. If it looks great here, it'll look great in the wild.

What to look for: clashing colors, invisible strokes (border color matching disc color), swag+border collision, glasses+brow overlap, ecoMark clipping. Anything that looks broken → we add a compat guard in the composer.
